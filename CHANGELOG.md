## [1.0.0] - 2026-01-15

### 🎉 Initial Release

#### Added
- **15+ AI Modules** for comprehensive startup validation
  - AI Idea Analyzer
  - Market Demand Analysis (TAM/SAM/SOM)
  - Competitor Intelligence
  - Gap Finder
  - Validation Score Calculator
  - Revenue Prediction Engine
  - Business Model Generator
  - SWOT Analysis
  - Investor Readiness Score
  - Risk Detection
  - Business Plan Generator
  - Pitch Deck Generator
  - AI Mentor Chatbot
  - Funding Recommendation Engine
  - Success Probability Predictor

- **Export Functionality**
  - PDF export (Pitch Deck, Business Plan, Full Report)
  - Word export (Business Plan)
  - Image export (PNG per slide)
  - JSON export (Data & Analytics)

- **Financial Modeling**
  - MRR, ARR, Gross Margin calculations
  - Burn Rate & Runway analysis
  - LTV:CAC Ratio
  - Break-even analysis
  - Sensitivity analysis (3 scenarios)
  - Valuation calculator
  - Cap table generator

- **Customer Discovery Tools**
  - Customer persona generator (4 industries)
  - Interview question generator
  - Survey templates
  - Landing page copy generator
  - Validation score calculator

- **Task & Action Planning**
  - Auto-generated action plans (40+ tasks)
  - Week-by-week breakdown
  - Quick wins identification
  - 5 validation checklists
  - Milestone time estimates

- **Analytics Dashboard**
  - Score trends over time
  - Industry breakdown
  - Performance metrics
  - Report card (A-F grades)
  - Benchmark percentiles

- **History Management**
  - Auto-save to localStorage
  - Search & filter functionality
  - Export history as JSON
  - Individual delete options

- **UI/UX Features**
  - Dark/Light theme toggle
  - Keyboard shortcuts (⌘H, ⌘B)
  - Toast notifications
  - Error boundaries
  - Responsive design
  - Mobile-optimized
